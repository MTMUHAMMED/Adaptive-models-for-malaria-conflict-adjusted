# R code for an Adaptive Gaussian Random Field (AGMRF) 
Attached are the codes for the work entitled "Bayesian Spatial Modeling of Conflict-Adjusted Under-Five Malaria Prevalence in
Nigeria" by M.T. Muhammed et al.  
The codes are:
A. "1.Conflict zone.R": Here we clean and categorize the ACLED data for our work into various conflict zones and visualized
B. "2. Sensitivity Conflict Grouping.R": Here were merged our ACLED data to NMIS data based on 5km, 10km and 15km buffer one after the other. Likewise the continues and categorical grouping of conflict were made.
C. "3. Sensitivity models and camparison": Here we model our data and check various metrics and mappings. 

Generally, the code obtained from data derived from the 10km buffer is available. To get other buffer or time window, refer to "2. Sensitivity conflict Grouping.R" and adjust the buffer or time window.
